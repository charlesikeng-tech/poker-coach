How it works

From your hand history to your work plan, in three steps.

  1. Get your hands

    Your room records every hand played. Download your history folder and compress it to .zip.

  2. Import them

    Drop the .zip. Duplicates are ignored, damaged files flagged: you can import the same folder every week.

  3. Read what matters

    Your stats by position and your leaks ranked by impact. With Elite, a debrief of the hands that decided each tournament.

Features

What a coach does, on all your hands.

Built for micro and low stakes, where the same mistakes cost the most.

Leak Detection

VPIP, open, 3-bet, c-bet, fold to 3-bet… by position and by stack depth, compared to solid player benchmarks. Only gaps frequent enough to matter are flagged. With Elite, month-by-month evolution of each stat to see if your leaks are closing.

Session Debrief Elite

After each tournament or cash session, the story and an opinion on each key moment: well played, mistake, or variance when the calculated equity says you were ahead.

Monthly Report Pro

Three priorities drawn from your leaks, and each month a coach review of what moved in your game.

Ranges & Training Pro

Your actual opening ranges vs. the benchmarks, and a quiz on your own spots: the ones you missed come back until you play them right.

Hand Replay

Replay any hand at the table, street by street, with stacks in big blinds.

Bankroll & Performance

ROI, ITM, results by buy-in and format, bankroll curve with your deposits and withdrawals.

Poker Tools

Four built-in calculators, accessible without a subscription: ICM (monetary equity by stacks and prizes), Pot Odds / EV (call profitability), M-Ratio (stack autonomy vs. blinds) and Range Equity (range comparison on a 13×13 grid, Monte Carlo simulation).

The numbers come from your hands. The AI explains, it invents nothing.
  • Every stat is calculated by NutsIQ on your imported hands, with the number of spots behind it.
  • The AI coach receives these numbers and anonymized hands: never your username or your opponents'.
  • No "GTO" promise: benchmarks from winning players, and what you can fix from your next session.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which rooms are supported?

Winamax (MTT, cash game, Sit & Go), Betclic (cash game, MTT soon) and PMU Poker (cash game) are available today. PokerStars is coming next: NutsIQ is built to support more rooms and formats.

Which buy-ins are supported?

All: NutsIQ imports every tournament regardless of its price, and sorts your results by buy-in bracket. Leak and range benchmarks are written for micro and low stakes (up to around €25); above that, they remain useful but less demanding than your field.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything happens in the browser: you import the .zip of your history folder. A desktop app that imports automatically after each session is planned.

Does NutsIQ play for me or is it used during games?

No. NutsIQ analyzes already-played hands, between sessions. It displays nothing while you're playing.

What happens to my data?

Your hands are used only to calculate your statistics. You can download everything or delete your account at any time from the Account page; deletion wipes everything. The AI coach only receives numbers and anonymized hands.

What happens if I cancel my subscription?

Your plan stays open until the end of the paid period, then your account reverts to Starter. Nothing is deleted: you'll find your full history if you come back.

How do AI credits work?

Each coach text has a real cost, so each plan has monthly credits: a leak explanation costs 1 credit, a report 2, a tournament debrief 5. With Ultimate, 1,000 credits and unlimited reviews for the most active grinders. Credits reset on the 1st of each month.
