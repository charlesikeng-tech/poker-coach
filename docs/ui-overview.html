NutsIQ — UI Overview

Screenshots of every screen. Generated from the running app with mocked data.

Access

Sign-in

Entry point for new and returning users. Google OAuth or email + password. Switches to sign-up mode inline.

Forgot password

Sends a reset link to the address on file. Works even for accounts originally created with Google.

Dashboard

Dashboard

Rolling summary of results: profit, ROI, ITM, cumulative profit curve, recent tournaments, cash game figures.

Results

Tournaments

Paginated list of all imported tournaments with results, buy-ins, finish positions and hand coverage.

Cash Sessions

List of cash game sessions with net BB, net money and hand counts. Master–detail layout.

Performance

Profit and ROI broken down by buy-in band, tournament type, speed, month and day of week.

Bankroll

Balance curve, deposit/withdrawal history, ROI confidence interval and buy-in limit rule.

Analysis

Statistics

Full stat line (VPIP, PFR, RFI, steal, 3-bet, C-bet…) overall and by position, with phase and month breakdowns.

Leaks

Stats outside the reference range, with confidence intervals. Confirmed vs. possible leaks. AI explanation on demand.

Ranges

Opening and defence ranges by position and stack depth, derived from the player's own hand history.

Improvement

Weekly plan

2–3 priorities drawn from recent leaks, frozen for the week. Tracks in-week progress and drill completion.

Training

Opening and sizing drills tied to the weekly plan. Instant feedback with reference ranges.

Settings & Account

Import

Drag-and-drop upload for hand histories and tournament summaries. Supports Winamax and PokerStars formats.

Subscription

Plan details, AI credits used this month, upgrade and billing portal access.

Account

Display name, email, password change, full GDPR data export and account deletion.

Privacy

What is stored, why, by whom, and how long. Links to export and account deletion.
